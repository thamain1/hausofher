05 Meet the Founder

Every woman deserves a seat she never has to fight for.

Latasha Varnadoe-Iloabuchi has spent her career building the rooms to prove it. As the founder of Haus of Her, she creates gatherings where women are honored, empowered, and respected exactly as they are — complete with crowns.

As Co-Founder and President of The 1895 Project, she reimagines the African American debutante tradition as a launchpad, with a bold goal: every student graduates high school, pursues education beyond it, and finishes debt free.

Her résumé also has a side that surprises people. With more than 20 years of accounting experience across real estate, construction, and development, she knows how to turn complex numbers into confident decisions. As Founder and CEO of The Varnadoe Group, she brings that same strategy — and a lot of heart — to the businesses she advises.

She is also a sought-after speaker and a playwright. Her first stage play, Queen Broken, which she co-wrote, debuts in fall 2027. A proud member of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated, she carries a lifelong commitment to sisterhood and service into everything she builds.

Strategist. Founder. Storyteller.Latasha Varnadoe-Iloabuchi — Founder, Haus of Her
