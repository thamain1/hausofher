01 The Haus

A house built for her, by women who rise together.

Haus of Her is a sisterhood where women are celebrated before they are asked to perform, crowned before they are asked to conform. Through brunches, book launches, and gatherings across Dallas, we create rooms where her story is the main event — honored, empowered, and respected, exactly as she is.

The crest of the Haus — every chair a throne
